# FileDock 1.36.4

- 修复单个或多个文件在同一页面无法拖入文件夹的问题；网格、列表和分栏视图均支持。放在文件夹中央移入，按住 ⌥ 复制；边缘仍显示排序插入线。
- 收藏、暂存中的真实文件夹也可接收文件；文件夹自身及其子目录不能成为自身的移入目标。
- 文件移动、复制、重命名、新建文件夹、移到废纸篓、标签与样式、制作副本／替身／压缩，以及暂存、收藏和文件排序接入统一撤销历史。⌘Z 撤销，⇧⌘Z 重做；多文件操作合为一步，新操作不再清掉之前的撤销记录。
- 普通大文件与多文件目录的移动无需扫描内容即可撤销；复制和新建项的撤销进入可恢复的废纸篓。恢复原位置时不会覆盖同名文件，遇到冲突可处理后重试。
- 切换标签时，底部类型筛选条的底板和选中项从原位置平滑过渡，文字短淡入并轻移。基准约 360ms，最快设置也保留可感知的过渡，保留「…」入口对齐和即时操作。
- 保留已有标签、颜色和文件内容微动画及拖放后的高亮反馈。

## English

- Fixes single and multiple files failing to drop into folders in the same panel, across grid, list and column views. Drop in a folder's center to move, or hold Option to copy; its edges retain insertion guidance for reordering.
- Real folders in Favorites and Staging can receive files too. A folder cannot be dropped into itself or its descendants.
- Unifies undo history for moving, copying, renaming, creating folders, trashing, tags and folder styles, duplication, aliases, archives, staging, favorites and file ordering. Use Command-Z to undo and Shift-Command-Z to redo; a batch is one step, and new operations retain earlier undo steps.
- Ordinary large-file and large-directory moves can be undone without scanning their contents. Undoing copies and newly created items uses recoverable Trash. Restoring an original location never overwrites an existing item; resolve a conflict and retry.
- Animates the type-bar background and selected segment continuously from their previous positions, with a visible text reveal. The baseline is about 360ms, retaining a perceptible transition even at the fastest setting, with the More anchor aligned and controls immediately usable.
- Retains tab, color and file-content micro-animations and post-drop highlighting.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel. Signed, not yet notarized by Apple.

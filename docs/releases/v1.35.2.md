# FileDock 1.35.2

- 浅色模式不再显示刘海黑色融合渐变，紧凑音乐栏也随浅色界面显示。
- 深色模式保留原有刘海融合效果；展开过冲与拖放行为保持不变。

## English

- Removes the black notch-blending gradient in light mode and lets the compact music bar follow the light appearance.
- Preserves dark-mode notch blending, expansion overshoot and file-drop behavior.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel. Signed, not yet notarized by Apple.

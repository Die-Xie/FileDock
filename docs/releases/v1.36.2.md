# FileDock 1.36.2

- 打磨标签选中底板的连续滑动，与标签滚动使用一致的速度曲线。
- 切换工作区或目录时，首批文件轻微上移并淡入；快速连续切换不反复闪动，后续批次和后台刷新不重播动画。
- 底部颜色与文件类型筛选也使用短促的选中底板过渡，筛选后的文件同步轻移、淡入。
- 合并连续切换标签产生的配置保存，复用已排序的目录缓存，减少较大工作区切换时的主线程开销。
- 扩大目录快照缓存，并以条目数、估算字节数和有效期限制占用；命中缓存后仍在后台刷新文件状态。
- 动效不改变文件布局与拖放目标；拖放、缩放和“减少动态效果”下立即完成内容呈现。

## English

- Refines the continuous tab selection highlight and aligns tab scrolling with its motion curve.
- Gently lifts and fades in the first file batch when switching workspaces or directories. Rapid switches share the current transition; subsequent batches and background refreshes do not replay it.
- Adds short selection transitions to the bottom color and file-type filters, with matching file-content motion.
- Coalesces selection-only configuration saves and reuses pre-sorted directory snapshots to reduce main-thread work during switching.
- Expands the directory snapshot cache with entry, estimated-byte and lifetime limits, while continuing to refresh cached folders in the background.
- Keeps file geometry and drop targets stable. Content transitions settle immediately during dragging, resizing or Reduce Motion.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel. Signed, not yet notarized by Apple.
